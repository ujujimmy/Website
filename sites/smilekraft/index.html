Why patients keep coming back

No upselling. No rushing. Just the treatment you actually need.

Plenty of people arrive at Smilekraft having put a problem off for months — usually because they were worried about the pain, or about what it would cost. So that's where we start: we look, we show you what we've found, and we tell you the price before anything begins.

If a tooth can be saved with a simple filling, we won't propose a crown. If a treatment can wait, we'll say so. It's a small clinic, which means you see the same faces each visit and nobody is trying to move you along.

  • Sterilisation you can watch. Instruments autoclaved between patients, disposables where disposables belong.
  • Painless technique. Topical numbing before the injection, and time given for it to work.
  • Modern diagnostics. Digital X-rays and intraoral imaging, so you see what we see.
  • Written estimates. The full cost of a treatment plan, up front, in writing.
  • Follow-up that's real. A check-in after major work, not just a bill.

Before you come in

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome and we'll always try to see you, but booking ahead means far less waiting. If you're in pain, call first and say so — we keep room in the day for urgent cases.

Will the treatment hurt?

Modern local anaesthetic does its job. We apply a numbing gel before the injection and wait for it to take full effect, so most patients feel pressure but not pain — root canals included. Tell us if you're anxious; we'll go slower and explain each step.

How much does treatment cost?

It depends on what's needed, so we examine first and then give you a written estimate covering the whole plan. Nothing starts until you've seen that number and agreed to it.

How long does a root canal or implant take?

Most root canals are finished in one or two sittings. An implant is placed in a single appointment, but the bone needs a few months to fuse with it before the crown goes on — we'll map out the dates at the start.

At what age should a child first see a dentist?

Around their first birthday, or whenever the first teeth appear. Early visits are short and mostly about getting comfortable in the chair, which pays off for years afterwards.

Do you handle emergencies outside clinic hours?

Call the clinic number and leave a message or a WhatsApp. We check it regularly and will get back to you with the earliest slot — often the same day.
